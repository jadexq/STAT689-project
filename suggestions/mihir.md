2. In the Library, show which course readings I haven't opened yet: list unread ones first with a "new" marker, and show a count like "2 of 5 readings opened".
3. Add group chats in a chat panel that works from anywhere on the campus. Any student can start a group chat for brainstorming on an activity, and all students can see the list of open group chats and join any of them. Messages are kept between sessions. Any member can download the chat. Only the creator can delete the group for everyone (the instructor can also remove a group if needed). If the students are stuck or have questions, they can tag the instructor, and the instructor can answer their questions in the group chat.
4. Warn before the idle disconnect. After 15 minutes without keyboard or click activity the tab disconnects without warning, which can happen while a student is reading course material in another tab, or following an active group chat without typing. Show a "Still there?" warning a minute or two before disconnecting, so students can stay connected with one click.
5. Add quick navigation for accessibility. A simple "Go to" menu (Library, Computer Lab, TA Office, my office) would help students on trackpads, small screens, or using assistive technology reach what they need quickly.
