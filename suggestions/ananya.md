# Suggestions: Ananya

1. Add a short welcome and a help button. The only instructions are in small text at the bottom of the screen. A new student sees a map and a chat box and may not know what to do first or where the TA is.

2. Make it clear who the virtual students are and what they do. I saw blue circles in the offices, but I could not tell if they were the students, and nothing happened when I walked up to them. Adding name labels and a greeting when you enter their room would show they are there to talk to.

3. Explain why the Classroom and Prep Room are closed and when they will open. They look closed with no reason, so students may think something is broken.

4. Let the TA also explain how to use the website, like moving, rooms, and chat. When I typed "help", it only talked about the course project steps.

5. Show a clear message when something is loading or has failed. The Library showed "Fetching reading list..." for a long time, and the chat said "Connection lost" at one point. A student cannot tell whether to wait or refresh.

6. Make it faster to get to the TA Office, for example with a "Go to TA Office" button. Clicking it from far away did not work for me, and walking tile by tile is slow.
