# Website Suggestions

The following suggestions focus on practical improvements to the current virtual campus. They are separate from the Classroom agent, agent-to-agent communication, and concurrent Virtual TA interactions proposed in `jade.md`.

## 1. First-Time Welcome Guide and Help Button

**Suggestion:** Show a short welcome guide on the student's first visit explaining the arrow/WASD controls, the minimap, the purpose of key rooms, and how to reach the Virtual TA. Add a **Help** button so students can reopen the guide whenever needed. The guide can be dismissed after reading.

**Benefit:** New students can learn to navigate the website without needing assistance.

## 2. Collapsible Sidebar Sections

**Suggestion:** Add expand/collapse controls to these sections. For example, a student could minimize Chat while exploring the campus and reopen it when needed. Keep the current chat content when the panel is collapsed.

**Benefit:** Students can focus on the campus and see a less cluttered interface.

## 3. Restore and Export Virtual TA Conversations

**Suggestion:** In the **Virtual TA interface in the TA Office**, show the student’s most recent TA exchanges when they return, if those messages are saved. Add an **Export TA Conversation** button that downloads their TA conversation as a Markdown (`.md`) file, clearly labeling the student and the Virtual TA. **This feature is for Virtual TA conversations only, not the general same-room chat shown in the campus sidebar.**

**Benefit:** Students can continue previous discussions and keep explanations or study notes for future reference.

## 4. Recent Announcements Preview

**Suggestion:** Enhance the existing Announcements Board section in the sidebar to show the latest three to five announcements, including their titles and dates. Clicking an item should open its details or take the student to the relevant board. If no announcements are available, keep the current empty-state message.

**Benefit:** Students can notice course updates quickly without searching through multiple rooms.
