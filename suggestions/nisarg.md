1. Cite course materials in TA answers. When the Virtual TA answers a question about a reading, it should cite the specific material and section it drew from, with a link that opens that passage on the Library shelf. Students can then verify the answer and read further, instead of taking the TA's word for it.
2. Show unread notices for bulletin boards. Right now students only see a pinned announcement if they walk into the Library or Computer Lab. A small badge on the minimap or chat panel ("2 new posts in the Library") would make sure important announcements are not missed.
3. Add personalized review quizzes. The TA already logs each student's questions in the Coach digest. It could use those to generate a short practice quiz on the concepts that student struggled with, so review is targeted rather than generic.
4. Allow rubric-aware self-review before submission. Let students run the Review skill on their own pull request in the Computer Lab against a rubric the instructor posts, so they get line-referenced feedback and can fix issues before the final submission.
5. Export a study packet. Let students download their TA conversation, in-class notes, and question digest for a topic as a single Markdown or PDF file, so they can study offline and keep a record after the course ends.
